We study spatial reasoning from paired egocentric observations and 3D scene geometry. The repository already contains a two-stage pipeline: first, per-view images and visible point-cloud subgraphs are aligned through a shared autoencoder latent; second, latent observations from random walks are matched against a coarsened global point-cloud graph for localization. We outline a paper around this pipeline with an emphasis on visibility-aware supervision, cross-modal latent alignment, and graph-based scene reasoning.
