\section{Experiments}

The repository already suggests a two-level experimental plan.

\paragraph{Representation learning.}
Measure image reconstruction, point-cloud reconstruction, latent alignment, and the effect of visibility-derived conditioning. Useful ablations are image-only, point-cloud-only, and no latent-alignment training.

\paragraph{Localization.}
Evaluate translation and view-direction error for latent-to-map matching, including robustness to partial observations and different random-walk trajectories.

\paragraph{Recommended metrics.}
For geometry: Chamfer distance, color consistency, and any downstream retrieval/localization accuracy. For localization: translation error, angular error, percentile summaries, and recall at distance thresholds. The existing `paper.md` also suggests adding runtime and partial-map robustness sweeps.
